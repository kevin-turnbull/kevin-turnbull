## Problem

Security reviews arrived late, used inconsistent checklists, and blocked launches without offering implementable defaults.

## Constraints

- Small central security engineering capacity
- Multiple stacks across teams
- Audit evidence needed for enterprise customers

## Approach

Codified threat-model templates, authz patterns, and secret-handling baselines as reusable architecture kits. Reviews checked deviation, not invention.

## Decisions

- Standardize on a small set of auth patterns instead of allowing one-offs
- Treat documentation and reference implementations as first-class deliverables
- Measure adoption, not only policy publication

## Outcomes

- Review cycle time shortened
- Critical findings concentrated earlier in design
- Product managers gained predictability around security work

## Role

Defined the pattern library, coached teams through first adoption, and aligned audit narratives with engineering reality.
