## Problem

Synchronous order workflows collapsed under campaign traffic. Retries amplified load, and support absorbed the customer impact.

## Constraints

- Payment provider contracts could not change mid-season
- Downstream inventory systems were eventually consistent
- Team capacity limited large rewrites

## Approach

Introduced an orchestration boundary with explicit state, idempotent handlers, and back-pressure. Separated customer acknowledgment from irreversible side effects.

## Decisions

- Accept eventual consistency for inventory confirmation with clear UX messaging
- Make retries policy-driven and observable
- Prefer outbox patterns over fragile dual writes

## Outcomes

- Peak error budget burn dropped materially
- Incident bridges shortened because state became inspectable
- Product could run campaigns without emergency architecture freezes

## Role

Led design workshops, authored ADRs, and paired with service owners through the first production cutovers.
