## Problem

Multiple product lines had grown independent runtimes, conflicting release trains, and duplicated operational tooling. Architecture reviews repeatedly rediscovered the same failure modes.

## Constraints

- No multi-quarter freeze on feature delivery
- Existing teams kept ownership of domain logic
- Compliance evidence had to remain continuous
- Migration risk had to be reversible in early phases

## Approach

Established a thin platform spine: shared identity, observability contracts, deployment primitives, and golden paths. Domain services adapted behind stable interfaces rather than being rewritten.

## Decisions

- Prefer contracts and paved roads over a single monorepo mandate
- Sequence migrations by blast radius, not by org chart preference
- Keep a dual-run window with explicit exit criteria

## Outcomes

- Release cadence stabilized across teams
- Mean time to diagnose cross-service incidents improved
- Architecture reviews shifted from tool debates to trade-off design

## Role

Owned target architecture, migration sequencing, and decision records; coached team leads through adoption without centralizing all implementation.
